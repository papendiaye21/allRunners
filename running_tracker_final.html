App

allRunners

A simple running log for distance, time, pace, and date—built for tracking workouts in one place.

  • Use Home to add runs and preview pace.
  • Runs lists everything you’ve logged this session; delete from there when you need to.
  • Search finds runs by date.
  • Map shows OpenStreetMap and can center on your GPS position.
  • Log mirrors the compact activity feed in a scrollable panel.

Connect Smashrun below to store runs in your account across sessions. Pace is shown as minutes per mile.

Privacy & data

Smashrun: distance, time, dates, notes, and activity metadata sync through your OAuth session to Smashrun’s servers when you connect. This browser: optional GPS trails and sync watermarks stay in local storage on this device only—they do not leave your machine unless you copy them elsewhere. Clear site data or another browser means trails and unsynced local hints may disappear.

Smashrun sync (runs backend)

allRunners stores runs through Smashrun once connected. Use Connect to authorize this app, then your add/view/delete run actions sync with Smashrun APIs.
