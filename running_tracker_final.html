App

allRunners

A simple running log for distance, time, pace, and date—built for tracking workouts in one place.

  • Use Home to add runs and preview pace.
  • Runs lists workouts saved on this computer; delete from there when you need to.
  • Search finds runs by date.
  • Map shows OpenStreetMap and can center on your GPS position.
  • Log mirrors the compact activity feed in a scrollable panel.

Runs are saved on this computer even if Smashrun is disconnected. Connect Smashrun below when you want those runs copied to your account. Pace is shown as minutes per mile.

Privacy & data

This computer: distance, time, date, and notes are stored in a local database (data/runs.sqlite) so a workout is kept without Smashrun. Smashrun: the same fields are copied to your Smashrun account only after you connect. This browser: GPS trails, personal reminders, and the private note log stay in local storage on this device. They are not sent to other people.

Smashrun sync (runs backend)

Smashrun is an optional sync. Runs already save on this computer. Connect to copy them to your Smashrun account.
